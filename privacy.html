Privacy Policy: Smudge Pot Uploader

Last updated: October 19, 2026

What the App accesses

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the App receives only what TikTok's login grants for the requested permissions: basic profile information (user.info.basic) and permission to upload videos as drafts (video.upload).

How it is used

This access is used only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's TikTok drafts and to check the upload's status. The App does not read, collect or store anyone else's data.

Where it is kept

Login tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent anywhere except to TikTok. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising or tracking.

How to remove access

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions → Smudge Pot Uploader → Remove. Deleting the App's local token file also removes it from the owner's computer.

Children

The App is not directed to children and is used only by its adult owner.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
