Smudge Pot Studio

Smudge Pot Studio is a one-person creative studio making short AI-assisted 3D animation and science-fiction videos.

Smudge Pot Uploader is a personal tool that uploads the studio's own finished videos from the owner's computer to the owner's own TikTok drafts. The owner then adds captions and sound and posts them in the TikTok app.
